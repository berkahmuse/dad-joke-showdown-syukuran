Entry for the [Dad Joke Showdown](https://make.fun/competitions/u-mfnakmal-dad-joke-showdown-1d2ce3e5)
bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun): one dad joke, at most 200 characters,
written to be told out loud at the dinner table.

## The joke

> I told the bread I was feeling crumby. It said, 'Don't worry, Dad — it's the yeast I can do.'

93 characters. The setup pun "feeling crumby" (crummy + bread crumbs)
primes the theme, and the punchline turns "the least I can do" into
"the yeast I can do" — the turn is understood on the first read, the
last word is the pun, and the whole thing reads aloud in one breath.
Different angle from the usual family-asks-a-question setup: here the
father talks to the bread itself, and the bread answers back.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the joke to `/output/entry.txt` (no trailing newline, so the
file holds exactly the 93 characters of the joke). Run it locally with:

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/dadjoke-out python3 main.py
```
